## Real Data

### Confirmed Signals

**Funnel breakage is at the handoff, not the top.** Wilkerson brings candidates in through outreach and events, but *"once I give them information and they apply online, that's where my communication with them ends."* There is no system to pass her qualitative notes ("soft ratings," impressions) to Professional Standards, who run background investigations. *"There's no system of record when you're doing early [notes]."*

**Applications are routed through a central county system that doesn't distinguish departments well.** All applications flow through "Big County" (Hennepin central HR) regardless of which department a candidate wants. Binary yes/no screening questions can auto-disqualify qualified candidates: *"depending on where you check and how you answer... it could send you a, oh, I'm so sorry, but you're not qualified, even though they are well qualified."*

**Unconventional recruiting works and is already happening.** Two mall hiring fairs (Mall of America, Ridgedale) with on-site application and interview produced 90 hires in one push, a real, specific, past success story, not a hypothetical.

**Outreach-to-applicant-tracking is manual.** Names collected at events (e.g., MACV military outreach) go onto a physical list Wilkerson keeps and are separately sent to HR, with no confirmed integration into whatever system HR uses downstream. *"You have a physical list... but then you have a list that's coming through the chain of the web somewhere... spreadsheets."*

**A real technology procurement event happened recently and was mentioned in passing.** The department is migrating from Guardian Tracking to Axon for performance/records tracking (a peer-tracking tool, adjacent to but distinct from recruitment).

**Retention is a people/culture problem, not a tooling gap.** Described as *"a revolving door"* driven by burnout and PTSD, not by any system inefficiency. A partner's death from alcohol-related causes after suppressing trauma was shared as a specific, severe example.

### Workarounds
- Physical/mental note-keeping of candidate impressions instead of a shared system.
- Manual list transfer to HR after recruiting events.
- Cross-department referrals when a candidate isn't a Sheriff's Office fit, an informal, relationship-based workaround rather than a system.

### Spending / Technology Investments
- Guardian Tracking → Axon migration confirmed but not explored for cost or decision process.
- No recruitment-specific software named or confirmed in use; the direct question about what software she uses on her computer went unanswered, deflected to an offer to connect Marley with Professional Standards.

### Solution-Seeking
- No confirmed search for recruitment-funnel software specifically.
- The mall hiring fair is a real, creative, non-software workaround the office found on its own.

### Emotional Signals
- Resignation about the volume of BS candidates endure emotionally on the job, tied to why retention is hard, not why recruiting tools are missing.
- Warmth and personal connection once Marley's grandfather (Norm Hansen, Apple Valley PD) came up.
- Genuine enthusiasm about problem-solving: *"It's been a long run, but I too love problem solving... a lot of it is just streamlined communication."*
