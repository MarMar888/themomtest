## Real Data

### Confirmed Signals

**Sales/use tax classification is a real, recurring, emotionally-charged pain.** Wisconsin Department of Revenue rules force him to distinguish taxable service labor from non-taxable custom software work on the same job: *"if I build a custom script that diagnoses a problem, well, that's custom software. That's not sales taxable. But if I do service work on that same computer... that time technically is taxable."* Described auditors as unpleasant and inconsistent: *"they seem to think everything is taxable until you prove them wrong, even if they know better."*

**Time tracking is deliberately avoided, at a real cost.** He estimates *"at least a hundred hours of billable time"* lost unrecorded over 23 years, a direct, if rough, revenue-loss number. The avoidance is intentional: he attributes better on-the-job performance to not tracking time because of ADHD-related distraction sensitivity.

**Current back-office tool (FreshBooks) is actively decaying.** Switched from QuickBooks to FreshBooks for cost reasons; now reports broken sync, dead transactions, stale branding, and no updates: *"they don't do updates anymore. Half of things are broken."* Pays ~$400/year, and describes the checkout price silently increasing from a quoted $360.

**No IT-specific back-office tool is known to exist.** Asked directly whether IT-based accounting services exist: *"Not that I'm aware of."*

**He has already independently conceived the same product Marley is exploring.** Described wanting to build *"an independent contractor app"* covering time/billing, state-specific compliance tips, and AI-assisted diagnostics, unprompted, before Marley's own thesis was raised.

**A concrete willingness-to-pay number was given, but it's hypothetical.** *"Between 30 and 40 bucks a month would probably be an easy sell, especially if it did the bookkeeping too... I'd buy that."* Future-tense, no money or commitment attached.

### Workarounds
- Deliberately keeps time tracking mental/informal rather than systematized.
- Uses specific phrasing/language on invoices to stay classified as non-taxable where possible ("the magic words").
- Prices services at competitive market rate rather than low, citing pricing-mistake lessons from early in his career.

### Spending / Technology Investments

- Significant unspecified spend on AI tools (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude) for diagnostic work, described as his largest software cost category.
- Built his own internal ERP this past summer for his own business (headless QuickBooks plus custom front end and MCP integration) and reports being highly satisfied with it.

### Solution-Seeking

### Emotional Signals
