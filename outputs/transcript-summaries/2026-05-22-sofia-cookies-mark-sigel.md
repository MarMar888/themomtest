## Real Data

### Confirmed Problems

**No unified way to interact with distributors.** Everyone in the promotional products supply chain uses a different ERP or software. The result: constant back-and-forth to communicate products, services, and structure. "Ad hoc understanding is the status quo." This is a structural interoperability problem, not a one-off frustration.

**Lead sourcing is not important when you go through distributors.** Mark confirmed that top-of-funnel lead generation is not a pain point for Sofia's because their customer acquisition goes through distributors. "Developing top line leads is not important since they go through distributing." This is a relevant negative signal for the lead-sourcing-for-SMBs thesis.

**Marketing and nurturing is where the need is.** In contrast to top-of-funnel lead gen, nurturing existing distributor relationships and driving brand awareness was described as "strong" in terms of importance. The product positioning challenge: people default to standard promotional products (pens, notebooks) and Sofia's has to create reasons to consider cookie promotions instead.

### Workarounds

- Method + QuickBooks + sosInventory: current ERP/operational stack. Mark evaluated a $40K ERP replacement and ultimately decided it was not worth it. The existing stack is "good enough" at his scale.

### Spending / Technology Investments

- **ERP evaluation aborted:** Mark considered a $40K ERP investment and stopped the process when a six-figure total cost became clear. Stayed on Method + QuickBooks + sosInventory.
- No new technology investments in the conversation.

### Solution-Seeking
