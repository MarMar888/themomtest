# Big 3 Goals -- Matt Breen Follow-up
**Date:** 2026-06-18 | **Contact:** Matt Breen, Lead Data Scientist, Minnetonka School District
**Context:** Third conversation in the attendance/absenteeism thread. First time explicitly exploring Breen's data science workflow beyond attendance. EduClimber July 1 attendance features should now be live or evaluable.

---

## Q1 -- Does EDIA solve the need?
**Original:** Does EDIA solve the need/problem?
**Sharpened:** "When you looked at EDIA, was there anything it could do that you couldn't already do in EduClimber or Skyward?"
**Why:** Avoids opinion. Gets a specific capability gap instead of a thumbs up/down.

## Q2 -- What data requests can't be fulfilled?
**Original:** What data requests come most often that can't be fulfilled?
**Sharpened:** "Walk me through the last time someone came to you with a request you couldn't fulfill -- what happened?"
**Why:** Past-specific anchor. Forces a real story instead of a category list.

## Q3 -- Where is data underutilized?
**Original:** What areas of the school feel like data is underutilized or underreported?
**Sharpened:** "Is there a specific area where you know the data exists in Skyward but nobody is actually using it -- and you've thought 'someone should be doing something with this'?"
**Why:** Tightens a broad question into a specific observation from a data scientist who sees the whole district. Most valuable question of the three if he answers it well.
