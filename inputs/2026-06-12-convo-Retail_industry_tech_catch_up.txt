OVERVIEW
--------
Marley met with a contact from Bluebird (a retail/CPG brokerage) for coffee near a lake in the Minneapolis area. The conversation covered industry trends in retail brokerage, Marley's career plans, and personal catch-up. The Bluebird contact shared insights about technology-enabled retail services, AI adoption in their business, and gave Marley career advice about joining smaller companies and maintaining work-life balance.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

  - Marley's career trajectory: Plans to finish senior year at UW-Madison, then likely move to San Francisco for software engineering; will start job searching in early fall; wants to eventually start his own company
