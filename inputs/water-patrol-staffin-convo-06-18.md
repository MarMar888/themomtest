Marley conducted an informational interview with a Water Patrol lieutenant at the Hennepin County Sheriff's Office (22-year veteran, 4th year as lieutenant) to identify pain points in water patrol operations that could potentially be solved with software or technology. The conversation covered emergency response protocols on Lake Minnetonka, staffing challenges, and technology use in water patrol operations.

## Atmosphere

Informal and candid, with the lieutenant being surprisingly open about operational vulnerabilities despite initial wariness. The tone was conversational and mutually respectful—Marley positioned himself as a curious student from UW-Madison who grew up on the lake, which helped build rapport. The lieutenant expressed genuine frustration about staffing shortages and public perception of law enforcement, while also showing pride in his team's capabilities and equipment.

## Key Takeaways

- **Critical staffing shortage**: Water Patrol operates with only 9 full-time deputies (down from a goal of 12), often just 1-2 people per shift covering all of Hennepin County's lakes and rivers—not just Lake Minnetonka
- **Volunteer program collapse**: Used to have ~50 volunteers 15 years ago; now essentially gone, eliminating a key staffing buffer
- **Recruitment pipeline broken**: For the first time ever, they received zero summer staffing support from the jail (their traditional source of extra personnel) due to jail's own shortages
- **Shift coverage reality**: Often only 1.5 people working (one deputy + sergeant splitting shift), despite public perception of much larger presence
- **Technology is well-funded but underutilized**: They have top-tier drones, sonar, GPS, and radio systems; the lieutenant admits he's "not a techie guy" and struggles with rapid tech changes
- **Back-office/admin identified as potential tech opportunity**: The lieutenant noted technology makes "reports quicker, arrests quicker" but didn't see obvious gaps—though he mentioned this unprompted when Marley asked about pain points
- **Sonar operations for recovery missions**: Described as "totally different thing than rescue" where "everything slows down"—potential area for optimization
- **GPS/waypoint challenges**: Wind, intoxicated callers, and location accuracy create significant delays in water emergencies where 10 minutes is often the survival threshold
- **Public perception crisis**: Post-2020 riots and ongoing protests have devastated recruitment; the lieutenant actively discourages his own son from law enforcement
- **County-level recruitment**: Lieutenant has no involvement; handled by separate recruitment staff with flyers and traditional methods—potential disconnect between operational needs and recruitment strategy
