Improving school attendance systems
===================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Marley had a follow-up call with his father to debrief a recent conversation Marley had with Annalise, an organized and capable district employee who works under Marley's stepmother. The call covered two main topics: a sports update about upcoming games (including White Bear Lake vs. Edina semifinals that Marley's extended family plans to attend), and a deeper dive into school district attendance intervention challenges. Marley is actively exploring this space for potential startup opportunities, explicitly stating "I'm just trying to find problems to solve." He shared EDIA, a company doing automated text-based attendance communication, as something that aligns with his product vision. His father provided rich context about the district's current fragmented approach: moving toward weekly Student Attendance Review Board (SARB) meetings at elementary schools, using EduClimber as a data warehouse but not its full potential, and the tension between needing systematic processes versus agile, proactive communication. The district's plan remains somewhat ambiguous, with new attendance features from EduClimber coming July 1st and Marley's stepmother taking over oversight of attendance—creating a window for potential change.

----------
Warm, supportive, and intellectually engaging. Father is clearly proud of Marley's curiosity and entrepreneurial mindset, repeatedly praising his persistence. The conversation blends family catch-up (sports, wedding stories, the sixth grader's activities) with genuine problem-solving collaboration. Father treats Marley as a capable peer despite acknowledging this is "so far from directly impacting your own life right now."


KEY TAKEAWAYS
-------------
  - District attendance intervention is in flux: Moving from reactive to proactive model with weekly SARB meetings, but plan is still ambiguous and not yet systematic across buildings
  - EduClimber's limitations: Acts as data warehouse/SIS integration with Skyward; has underutilized smart forms and family communication features; new attendance features launching July 1st with unclear value
  - Two distinct problems identified: (1) systematic/process issues for managing chronic truancy cases, and (2) efficiency/agility for day-to-day attendance communication and early intervention
  - EDIA as reference point: Text-first, automated two-way communication with parents (excuse collection, intervention alerts, document upload) that Marley sees as directionally aligned with his product thinking
  - Key district personnel dynamics: Annalise is highly capable and organized but may lack strategic vision; current attendance lead is "pen and paper" style; Marley's stepmother taking oversight July 1st will likely increase urgency and openness to new systems
  - Marley's insight: Custom "business logic" around attendance patterns (e.g., timing of absences relative to school year start, correlating grades with attendance) may be differentiator versus out-of-box solutions
  - COVID as catalyst: Exposed and worsened attendance culture, creating district willingness to address long-standing gaps
