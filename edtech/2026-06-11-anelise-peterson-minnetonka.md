## Attendance Policy & State Requirements
- Unexcused absences: notice sent at 3 days, county report at 7 days (state-mandated).
- Excused absences do **not** count toward habitual truancy thresholds.
- Minnesota Dept. of Education flags chronic absenteeism at **18 days** (any mix of excused/unexcused).
- Schools must report to the county; county then contacts families for support.

## Current Attendance Workflow
- Teachers log daily attendance in **Skyward**.
- At 3 unexcused days → letter drafted (manual).
- At 7 unexcused days → letter sent to family **and** reported to county.
- At 12 & 17 days → additional letters/notifications.
- Excused-absence requests processed via paper form; principal approves.
- Paraprofessionals currently generate and mail letters (privacy concern).

## Issues & Pain Points
- **Inconsistency**: Some principals skip letters when vacations are approved, creating equity gaps.
- **Manual effort**: Three-click process in EduClimber; still need to enter a security code for families to view letters.
- **Low engagement**: Many families never open emailed letters → no proof of receipt.
- **Early-intervention gap**: Anxiety-related absences aren't flagged until the 7-day threshold, causing snowball effect.
- **Data privacy**: Paraprofessionals seeing all student records in EduClimber is not compliant.
- **System limitations**: EduClimber lacks true automatic email dispatch; Skyward is outdated for automation.

## Planned Improvements & Tools
- **Threshold scoring**: Assign points for excused vs. unexcused days; trigger alerts at 7 days, 10% attendance loss, and 17 excused days.
- **Smart forms**: Auto-attach to student record when threshold hit; track letters, interventions, parent contracts.
- **Flag system**: Flagged students appear on a live dashboard for real-time visibility, not just weekly reports.
- **Weekly attendance team meeting**: Review flagged students, decide on letter vs. intervention, update flags.
- **Intervention options**: Soft-start (late arrival), staff check-ins, snacks, attendance contracts, individualized plans.
- **Automation goal**: One-click "send to parent" within EduClimber, eliminating Skyward/snail-mail step.

## Additional Observations
- Affluent families often accumulate many **excused** absences, pushing students toward chronic absenteeism despite policy allowances.
- Early-year absences (first two months) are more critical than later-year "spring" absences.
- Staff roles: nurses lead letter sending; paraprofessionals assist but raise privacy concerns.
- Desire for a unified, searchable attendance case-management system that blends reporting, intervention tracking, and parental communication.
