# Research Links

## Absenteeism Rates & Market Size

- https://www.aei.org/research-products/report/lingering-absence-in-public-schools-tracking-post-pandemic-chronic-absenteeism-into-2024/
  AEI: national chronic absenteeism at ~23% in 2023-24 (9.4M students). Still 71% above pre-pandemic levels despite declining from 28% peak in 2021-22.

- https://www.the74million.org/article/k-12-chronic-absenteeism-rates-down-from-peak-but-remain-persistently-high/
  The 74: rates down from peak but persistently high nationally.

- https://www.returntolearntracker.net/
  Return to Learn Tracker: chronic absenteeism data 2017-2025, state by state.

- https://minnesotareformer.com/2024/04/01/the-topline-graduation-rates-and-chronic-absenteeism/
  Minnesota Reformer: Minnesota at 24.5% in 2023-24, above national average. Free/reduced lunch students at 32%, special ed at 32%.

- https://www.minnpost.com/other-nonprofit-media/2023/09/attendance-gap-new-data-from-minnesota-reveals-chasm-in-chronic-absenteeism/
  MinnPost: Minnesota absenteeism data and demographic disparities.

- https://www.attendanceworks.org/continued-high-levels-of-chronic-absence-with-some-improvements-require-action/
  Attendance Works: national overview, improvement trends, action framing.

## Parent Communication & Chronic Absenteeism

- https://ies.ed.gov/ies/2025/01/how-text-message-parents-reduce-chronic-absence-using-evidence-based-approach
  IES: text messaging parents reduces chronic absence. Evidence-based backing for the parent communication layer as a real intervention, not just an efficiency play.
